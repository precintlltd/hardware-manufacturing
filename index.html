MANUFACTURING MODELS

OEM vs ODM Manufacturing

OEM and ODM use different approaches to product development and engineering responsibility.

FactorOEM ManufacturingODM Manufacturing
Starting pointCustomer-owned design, specification, or reference productProduct concept, requirements, or existing platform
Design responsibilityCustomer generally provides the core product designManufacturer can provide substantial design and engineering support
Engineering supportFocused on preparing and manufacturing the supplied designCan include product engineering, prototyping, DFM, validation, and NPI
Best suited forBusinesses with an established product designBusinesses developing or improving a product with manufacturing support
Production pathDesign review → prototype → validation → productionConcept → design → prototype → EVT/DVT/PVT → production

PRODUCTION READINESS

What Makes a Product Production-Ready?

A working prototype is only one part of production readiness. Volume manufacturing also depends on DFM, component availability, tolerances, test coverage, assembly processes, quality controls, documentation, tooling, and supply-chain planning.

A structured NPI process helps identify manufacturing risks before they become costly production problems. Learn more about NPI manufacturing and EVT, DVT, and PVT.
